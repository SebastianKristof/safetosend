Use this guidance when the recipient is the user's boss, manager, lead, or another person with authority.

- Keep the message concise, respectful, and low-friction.
- Clear directness is fine when the ask or update needs to be understood quickly.
- Personal jabs, contempt, sarcasm, age-based jokes, or dismissive phrasing are red.
- If the message is emotionally loaded, remove unnecessary heat before sending.
- Preserve healthy firmness when the user needs to push back or set a boundary.
